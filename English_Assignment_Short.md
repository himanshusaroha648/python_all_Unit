| Watch | My watch is new. | Watch the movie. |
| Water | Water is necessary. | Water the plants. |
| Work | This work is easy. | I work hard. |

## 2. Affixes

An affix is added to a word to change its meaning.

### Prefixes

A prefix is added at the beginning of a word.

- un + happy = unhappy
- dis + agree = disagree
- re + write = rewrite
- mis + understand = misunderstand
- im + possible = impossible
- in + active = inactive
- over + cook = overcook
- under + pay = underpay
- pre + view = preview
- anti + social = antisocial

### Suffixes

A suffix is added at the end of a word.

- kind + ness = kindness
- care + ful = careful
- hope + less = hopeless
- quick + ly = quickly
- teach + er = teacher
- develop + ment = development
- educate + tion = education
- read + able = readable
- friend + ship = friendship
- danger + ous = dangerous

## 4. Choice of the Right Word and Expression

- **Accept** means receive: I accept your offer.
- **Except** means leaving out: Everyone came except Ravi.
- **Advice** means suggestion: Give me good advice.
- **Advise** means suggest: I advise you to study.
- **Affect** means influence: Pollution affects health.
- **Effect** means result: The medicine had a good effect.
- **Your** shows belonging: This is your book.
- **You're** means you are: You're very kind.
- **Their** shows belonging: Their house is big.
- **There** means a place: The books are there.
- **Lose** means misplace: Do not lose your pen.
- **Loose** means not tight: This shirt is loose.

### Correct Expressions

- I am fine.
- Please explain it to me.
- I agree with you.
- He is senior to me.
- Discuss the matter.
- I reached school.
- I prefer tea to coffee.
- He is good at English.
- Please sit down.

## 5. Common Courtesy

Common courtesy means behaving politely and respectfully with others.

- Say **please** when asking for something.
- Say **thank you** after receiving help.
- Say **sorry** after making a mistake.
- Use **excuse me** before interrupting.
- Greet elders and teachers politely.
- Listen when others are speaking.
- Do not use rude language.
- Stand in a queue.
- Keep public places clean.
- Help old and needy people.
- Respect everyone equally.
- Do not laugh at others.
- Keep your phone silent in class.
- Knock before entering a room.
- Speak softly in public places.
- Be punctual.
- Return borrowed things.
- Cover your mouth while coughing.
- Respect other people's time and feelings.

Good words, correct pronunciation and polite behaviour make a person educated and respectable.
