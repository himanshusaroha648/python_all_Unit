## 2. Affixes

Affixes are letters placed before or after a root word.

### Prefixes

- un + fair = unfair
- dis + honest = dishonest
- re + build = rebuild
- mis + lead = mislead
- non + sense = nonsense
- pre + school = preschool
- inter + national = international
- sub + way = subway
- super + human = superhuman
- under + ground = underground

### Suffixes

- dark + ness = darkness
- child + hood = childhood
- use + ful = useful
- fear + less = fearless
- slow + ly = slowly
- act + or = actor
- govern + ment = government
- enjoy + ment = enjoyment
- wash + able = washable
- nation + al = national

Affixes help us form new words and understand their meanings.

## 4. Choice of the Right Word and Expression

- **Brake** stops a vehicle; **break** means to damage something.
- **Buy** means purchase; **by** means near or through.
- **Compliment** means praise; **complement** completes something.
- **Desert** is a dry place; **dessert** is a sweet dish.
- **Later** means after some time; **latter** means the second of two.
- **Quite** means fairly; **quiet** means without noise.
- **Stationary** means still; **stationery** means writing material.
- **Principal** is a school head; **principle** is a rule.
- **All ready** means completely prepared; **already** means before now.
- **Everyday** means usual; **every day** means each day.

### Better Expressions

- Please lend me your pen.
- I have known him for two years.
- She is married to a doctor.
- He went to the market.
- I am interested in music.
- We discussed the problem.
- Please give me some information.
- She is afraid of dogs.
- I will meet you tomorrow.

Correct expressions make communication natural and easy to understand.

## 5. Common Courtesy

Common courtesy is the habit of treating people with respect.

- Wish people according to the time of day.
- Use polite words while speaking.
- Wait patiently for your turn.
- Give attention to the speaker.
- Ask permission before using someone’s things.
- Keep promises and arrive on time.
- Give credit for another person’s work.
- Hold the door open when suitable.
- Offer help to a person in need.
- Keep noise low in quiet places.
- Do not stare at people.
- Avoid gossip and hurtful remarks.
- Respect different opinions.
- Keep classrooms and roads clean.
- Follow rules in public places.
- Thank people for their time.
- Apologise honestly for mistakes.
- Speak politely even during disagreement.
- Keep confidential matters private.
- Leave a place as clean as you found it.

Good manners create friendship, trust and peace in society.
